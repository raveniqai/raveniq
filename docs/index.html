How we build

  • Your data stays with you. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Numbers you can trust. Calculations are done by ordinary, tested code. AI only helps with reading and explaining, and never invents the figures.
  • Works offline. Nothing important waits for a server.
  • Made for everyone. Large text, VoiceOver, light and dark.
